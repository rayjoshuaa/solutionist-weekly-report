Issues & notes — found while checking the platforms, 18 November 2026
YouTubeCause #33 was uploaded more than once — a full video and a #shorts cut with near-identical titles.
YouTubeOnly Causes #32–#34 carry the chapter number in the video title. Causes #1–#31 use topic titles only, so the videos are hard to match to book chapters.
YouTubeFour older videos (about 2 years old) use a different Cause numbering that clashes with the current series — in the old set, #4 is Emotional Baggage; in the book series, #4 is Ages & Stages.
LinkedInDuplicate posts: Causes #20 and #21 were each posted twice on the company page, and #15 and #16 twice on Jon's profile.
LinkedInCauses #1–#12 do not appear in the visible LinkedIn feed history (the feeds load about one year back, to Cause #13).
All platformsCause #21 repeats the title “Parenting Styles & Practices”, the same title as Cause #8 — it runs on the website, YouTube and the podcast.
WebsiteArticle headings are inconsistent: Causes #11, #12 and #33 have no “#”, and #14 and #31 have no colon.
Website · PodcastCause #34 came out of order: its website article was published 26 May 2026, a week before its podcast episode (2 Jun 2026).
