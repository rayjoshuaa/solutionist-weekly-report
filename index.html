How this report works

What Jon receives, and when.

  • Every week: this page is updated with what was published and a proof link or screenshot for each item.
  • End of month: a monthly report with every task, every link, page views and responses (Task 10 in the brief).
  • Problems are flagged early. If a deadline is at risk, Jon hears about it right away, with a new date — not after the fact.
  • One master list per chapter. Latest version only. Old drafts are archived, so nothing is ever published from the wrong file.
